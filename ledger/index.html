Can what a language model says about its own states count as evidence about those states?

Current open-weight language models can carry a decodable, behaviorally consequential internal state while their untrained self-reports do not reveal it beyond what the text itself shows. This does not show the state is conscious, but it means negative self-reports should not be treated as evidence of absence for these systems. The evidence is strongest for the pain direction studied in the OWMI battery and for the models tested.
